FAQs

How do I deposit funds?

Click Deposit on your dashboard, send Bitcoin to the admin BTC address shown, then submit the amount for admin approval. Bank and Visa are currently unavailable.

How long does deposit approval take?

An admin verifies your BTC payment on-chain and approves the deposit. You'll receive an email and in-app notification once credited.

How do I complete identity verification (KYC)?

Go to Settings → Identity Verification or Profile, and submit your SSN for verification.

Can I convert crypto to cash?

Yes. Use the Exchange page to convert between BTC and your cash balance instantly.

Report Fraud

If you suspect unauthorized activity on your account, report it immediately.
